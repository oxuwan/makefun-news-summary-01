November 11, 2026: Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka handed his resignation to President Prabowo after courts voided his education credentials, Reuters reported; 120 lawmakers seek impeachment. Russia and Ukraine agreed in principle the largest prisoner exchange of the war as US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended without a ceasefire, AP reported. Iran's top negotiator told Al Jazeera the Strait of Hormuz will 'never return' to its pre-war status until the US answers its seven conditions, sent via Qatar, after 37 ships were hit in October, the worst month of the war. Four million Spanish workers staged the first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, with marches in Madrid and Barcelona ending in clashes, Reuters reported. Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases — 11,042 infections and 5,387 deaths — the country's deadliest and the second-largest on record, AP reported. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free status, Reuters reported. UN chief Antonio Guterres told COP31 leaders in Turkey that the slow climate response had put the world on a 'path to destruction', Reuters reported. US stocks rebounded, with the S&P 500 up 1.3% to 7,619.38 and CoreWeave up 25%; Europe's STOXX 600 hit a record high, Reuters reported. Anthropic's bankers prepared to raise its IPO valuation to $1.5 trillion or more after strong demand for its 825-million-share offering at $70–$78, the Financial Times reported. Northwestern Polytechnical University, a Chinese military-linked university, bought two Nvidia H200 chips for about $123,000 despite a US blacklist, Reuters reported. Democrat Scott Colom beat Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's Senate race by 4,700 votes, the first Black senator from the state since Reconstruction, the AP reported. Hoax bomb threats to about 6,300 schools in Italy and 700 more in Scotland and Northern Ireland forced evacuations, the BBC reported; police assessed them as hoaxes.
