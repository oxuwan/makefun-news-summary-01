November 11, 2026: Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka resigned after courts ruled his education documents invalid, Reuters reported; 120 lawmakers seek impeachment and a successor must be chosen within 60 days. Russia and Ukraine agreed a large prisoner exchange as US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended without a ceasefire, AP reported. Iran's security chief said the Strait of Hormuz will stay shut until the US answers Tehran's seven conditions, and a top negotiator said it will 'never return' to its pre-war status; Al Jazeera reported at least 37 attacks on ships there in November. Millions of Spanish workers staged the first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, with marches in Madrid and Barcelona ending in clashes and at least 73 arrests, Reuters reported. Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases — 11,042 infections and 5,387 deaths — the country's deadliest and the second-largest on record, AP reported. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free status on December 23, Reuters reported. UN chief Antonio Guterres told COP31 leaders in Turkey that the slow climate response had put the world on a 'path to destruction'. US stocks rebounded, with the S&P 500 up 1.3% to 7,619.38 and the Nasdaq up 1.6%, as CoreWeave jumped 25%; Europe's STOXX 600 hit a record high, Reuters reported. Anthropic launched its Nasdaq listing roadshow, offering 825 million shares at $70–$78 for a valuation of about $1 trillion, Reuters reported. A Chinese military-linked university bought two Nvidia H200 AI chips for about $123,000 despite a US export blacklist, Reuters reported. British police detained a 41-year-old US soldier on suspicion of spying for Russia, the BBC reported. At the WTA Finals, 19-year-old Mirra Andreeva beat Aryna Sabalenka 6–4, 6–0, winning the last 11 games to reach the semis, Reuters reported.
